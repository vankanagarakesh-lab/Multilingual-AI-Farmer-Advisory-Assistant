# Agricultural Water Conservation & Efficient Irrigation Reference

Water Conservation & Low-Water Farming Facts:
- Efficient Irrigation Practices: Drip irrigation delivers water directly to crop root zones, reducing evaporation and runoff loss by 40-60%; sprinkler irrigation is effective for undulating terrain and closely spaced crops (pulses, groundnut).
- Drought & Low-Water Crop Options: In dry or water-scarce conditions, drought-tolerant crops include millets (ragi, bajra, jowar), pigeon pea (arhar/toor), groundnut, and seasonal pulses. Suitability depends on local soil type, agro-climatic zone, rainfall, and season.
- Moisture Conservation Methods: Mulching with crop residue or reflective sheets retains soil moisture; Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) in paddy saves water without yield loss.

- తక్కువ నీటితో సాగయ్యే పంటలు: చిరుధాన్యాలు (రాగులు, సజ్జలు, జొన్నలు), కందులు, పెసలు, వేరుశనగ మరియు పప్పుధాన్యాలు.
