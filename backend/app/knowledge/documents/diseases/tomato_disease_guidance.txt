# Tomato Crop Health & Yellow Leaves Reference

Tomato Yellow Leaves & Disease Management Facts:
- Common Causes: Overwatering or poorly drained soil causing root stress and nutrient uptake failure; Nitrogen and essential mineral deficiency (lower older leaves yellow first); Sucking pests such as whiteflies and aphids transmitting leaf curl virus; Early and late fungal blights (leaf spots with yellow halos).
- Effective Management: Check soil moisture before watering and provide adequate drainage to avoid root waterlogging. Prune and dispose of diseased lower leaves. Inspect undersides of leaves for sucking pests. Apply balanced NPK fertilizers in split doses based on soil requirements. Apply neem extract or certified bio-fungicides if fungal infection persists.
- Prevention & IPM: Maintain 60x45 cm plant spacing for sunlight and airflow. Practice crop rotation with non-solanaceous crops. Install 15-20 yellow sticky traps per acre for whitefly monitoring.
